ID: KB-001
Title: VPN will not connect from home
Text: If the company VPN will not connect from home, try these steps in order. 1. Check that your home internet works by opening any website. 2. Close the VPN client fully and open it again. 3. Update the VPN client to the latest version from the Software Center. 4. Try a mobile hotspot. If the VPN connects there, your home network may be blocking it. 5. If the error says gateway not reachable, contact the service desk with the error code and the time. Never share your password with anyone.
